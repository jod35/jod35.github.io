## My Experience

[PyCon Africa 2026](https://africa.pycon.org/) took place in Kampala, Uganda, my home country. I was fortunate to be part of the organizing committee, and it was an experience I truly enjoyed.

The conference brought together Python developers from across the continent and beyond. We learned a lot, shared ideas, made new connections, met old and new friends, and, of course, enjoyed some really good food.

I put together this short 10-minute video to give you a glimpse of what the conference was like.

## A Conversation with the Creator of Python

One of the highlights of the conference was having [Guido van Rossum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guido_van_Rossum), the creator of Python, join us for a fireside chat titled *The Evolution of Python: Lessons from Its Creator*.

It was a great opportunity to hear directly from the person behind a programming language that so many of us use every day. I'm glad I got to record the conversation and share it here.
